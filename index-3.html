How credits work

  • Referral bonus: $100 each for the first 2 recurring referrals, $150 each after that. It's earned after the client's second paid month.
  • Monthly credit: $10 for every full month each referral stays recurring. It stops when the lead is marked Cancelled.
  • Tiers: Insider at 1–2 recurring referrals, Ambassador at 3–9, Elite at 10+ (protocol free).
